About

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics from Mawlana Bhashani Science and Technology University (MBSTU), Tangail. My academic training covered survival analysis, regression modelling, Bayesian statistics and time series analysis, and I have applied these methods to large real-world datasets through independent projects.

My recent work focuses on public health and healthcare data. Using NHANES data linked to national mortality records, I followed 50,819 US adults and estimated mortality risk with survey-weighted Cox models and competing-risks methods. In a separate study, I analysed 30-day hospital readmission among 69,970 diabetic patients across 130 US hospitals.

For my final-year research, I evaluated monthly wind speed forecasting for Bangladesh, comparing SARIMA against five machine learning models on 43 years of NASA POWER data covering 64 districts. Diebold-Mariano tests showed that no model performed significantly better than SARIMA.

I work primarily in R and Python for statistical analysis and modelling, SQL (DuckDB) for data extraction and transformation, and Power BI for reporting. I build dashboards with DAX measures and Power Query so that reports refresh automatically as the data changes. I also use SPSS for survey analysis and LaTeX for technical writing.

I am seeking a data analyst position in public health or healthcare, where I can contribute to data analysis and reporting. In the longer term, I plan to pursue a master's degree in Data Science and Machine Learning.
